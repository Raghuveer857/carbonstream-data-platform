# 🚀 About This Project

CarbonStream is an enterprise sustainability analytics platform designed to collect, process, validate, analyze, and report carbon emissions across multiple facilities, manufacturing plants, distribution centers, and operational environments.

Organizations worldwide are increasingly required to monitor environmental impact, track greenhouse gas emissions, generate sustainability reports, and comply with ESG reporting requirements.

CarbonStream provides a centralized platform for collecting operational energy consumption data, fuel usage metrics, utility consumption records, and sustainability indicators while generating standardized carbon-emission calculations and ESG intelligence reports.

The platform demonstrates enterprise Data Engineering workflows focused on sustainability analytics, regulatory reporting, environmental intelligence, data quality management, and large-scale reporting infrastructure.
